03 / About BASURI

A little breath.
A world of music.

BASURI introduces children to the bansuri — a traditional Indian bamboo flute with a warm, expressive voice. Lessons are designed to make the first steps approachable, from holding the instrument correctly to creating a clear tone.

We focus on musical foundations, attentive listening and regular practice. Each learner can progress at a comfortable pace, with guidance that respects their age, experience and individual learning style.

07 / Meet the instrument

A simple flute.
A rich voice.

The bansuri is a side-blown flute traditionally made from bamboo. The player shapes the sound with breath and changes notes by opening and covering the finger holes.

Children are introduced to the instrument gently, including how to care for it and handle it safely.
